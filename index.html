A QUICK SEA GUIDE

How to play

Drag your finger or mouse from the first letter to the last. You can travel in any of eight directions, including backwards.

  • Progression: Clear sub-themes for mastery ranks, daily goals and shells. Level up to explore new islands and gain modifiers.
  • Waves: A row or column shifts each tide. Letters from found words never move; unfound words are rewoven so the puzzle stays solvable.
  • Pearls: Find two words within five seconds to earn one. Spend them on a freeze or a letter hint.
  • Mystery: After finding six words, solve the bonus riddle and find its hidden answer.
  • Keyboard: Focus the grid; move with arrow keys. Press Enter or Space at a starting letter, move, then press again to select.
